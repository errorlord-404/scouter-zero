## Overview
A very short Hindi-language reel touching on a key hidden job market insight: some companies shut down their public job postings and only hire through direct channels, keeping their open roles completely off job boards.

## Key Ideas & Insights
*Note: Transcription quality is limited due to audio being in Hindi with transliteration artifacts. Core message reconstructed from available content.*

- Certain companies (particularly in direct/private hiring mode) close off their job listings from public platforms
- These companies have open positions that are never posted anywhere publicly
- Jobs are filled through direct referrals, personal networks, or internal channels
- Insight framed as a competitive advantage — knowing about these roles gives you an edge over applicants who only check job boards

# 5 Ways I Use AI for Content Marketing
**Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reels/DYj0imohYWv/
**Creator:** Ava | Content Marketing | Social Media Management (@personalbrandlaunch)

## Overview
A practical walkthrough of 5 specific AI-powered workflows Ava uses in her real content marketing practice — from hook writing to story analytics. Each use case is precise and immediately actionable for personal brand builders and social media managers.

## Key Ideas & Insights

1. **AI for viral hooks**
   - Prompt AI to find "viral outlier hooks" in your specific niche
   - Request written hooks, verbal hooks, and visual hooks separately — three different formats for the same idea

2. **AI for viral scripts with fill-in-the-blank structures**
   - Feed AI multiple proven viral scripts (even from outside your niche)
   - Have AI break down *why* each script went viral
   - Ask AI to turn those scripts into fill-in-the-blank templates
   - Apply those templates to your own niche content

3. **AI for carousels from viral reels**
   - Take your own reels that already went viral
   - Have AI convert them into carousel posts
   - Logic: the topic is already proven — repurpose it into a different format

4. **AI for content ideas from real life**
   - AI listens to and transcribes your calls
   - AI reads your calendar each week
   - AI scans through your emails
   - Output: content ideas based on real lessons, experiences, and things you actually learned that week — authentic content rooted in lived experience

5. **AI for story iterations from analytics**
   - Feed AI your story performance analytics
   - AI identifies best-performing content
   - AI creates different iterations/versions of that top content to recreate its success

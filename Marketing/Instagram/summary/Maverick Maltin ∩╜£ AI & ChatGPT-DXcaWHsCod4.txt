# Build a Viral AI Instagram Account That Sells Digital Products
**Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reels/DXcaWHsCod4/
**Creator:** Maverick Maltin | AI & ChatGPT (@mavgpt)

## Overview
A step-by-step playbook for building a viral Instagram account using AI tools, modeled after real accounts already generating $100,000/month. The core idea: find a working niche persona, clone its content strategy with AI-generated scripts and videos, post aggressively, then monetize with a digital product.

## Key Ideas & Insights
- The "monk account" on Instagram is cited as the proof of concept — a faceless account selling a happiness e-book making $100k/month.
- The workflow is entirely replicable and requires no on-camera presence.

**Step-by-step process:**
1. Find a viral niche account as inspiration (e.g., the monk account)
2. Go to Claude → prompt it to analyze what the account talks about and why it goes viral
3. Define a persona (example: "wise surfer sitting on a surfboard, talks about wisdom, happiness, surfer philosophy")
4. Prompt Claude: "Write 50 viral scripts for this surfer persona similar to the monk account"
5. Take scripts to Higgsfield (AI video generation tool) → paste script → generate video
6. Create a new Instagram account for this persona
7. Download video from Higgsfield → upload to Instagram with a simple caption → post
8. Posting cadence: 5 videos per day, every 2 hours. Schedule them in advance.
9. Once traction builds, go back to Claude → prompt: "Make me a digital product — a Surfer Wisdom e-book"
10. Sell the e-book to the audience

## Tools Mentioned
- **Claude** — AI assistant used for account analysis, script writing, and digital product creation
- **Higgsfield** — AI video generation tool; paste a script, generate a video

## Examples & Case Studies
- **The Monk Account** — Faceless Instagram account making $100,000/month selling a "happiness e-book." Used as the benchmark and inspiration model for the entire strategy.
- **Wise Surfer persona** — Hypothetical persona the creator builds live as an example: surfboard, wisdom + happiness content, surfer philosophy angle.

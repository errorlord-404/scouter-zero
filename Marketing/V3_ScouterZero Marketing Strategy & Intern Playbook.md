## Version 3.0 | Team: Aniket (Manager) + 3 Interns

## What Changed from V2 to V3

V2 was about getting started. V3 is about getting systematic. Three things changed:

1. **AI-agent first.** Every repeatable marketing task now has an agent "seat" on the team. Humans own decisions; agents own execution.
2. **PLG-informed funnel thinking.** We now track and optimise all seven layers of the growth funnel — not just top-of-funnel follower counts.
3. **Product-Led SEO as a primary channel.** Social media is for awareness. Search is where high-intent job seekers actually look. We go after both.

---

## 1. What We Are Building & Why

ScouterZero is a curated job board for Product Managers. Our core differentiator is simple and powerful: **every listing includes the recruiter's direct LinkedIn profile and email address.** We bypass the ATS black hole entirely.

Jobs are scraped daily from LinkedIn and reviewed before going live. Coverage: Dubai, London, New York, United States, Remote, and global.

**Marketing goal:** Acquire PM job seekers organically through content, community, product-led SEO, and AI-powered automation — with zero paid spend.

**Long-term vision:** Build a fully automated content and acquisition pipeline that compounds over time and scales to future products.

**Why PLG applies to us:**
ScouterZero is already a product-led product. Anonymous users get two free LinkedIn profile reveals before being gated — that is a reverse trial in action. Every PM who lands on the site and experiences the value of direct recruiter access before signing in is going through a PLG funnel. Our marketing job is to drive more of the right people to that moment.

## 5. Content Strategy

### Core Framework: Hook → Content → CTA

Every single piece of content follows this structure:

- **Hook:** Stop the scroll in 0–3 seconds. Lead with tension, a bold stat, or a relatable pain point.
- **Content:** Deliver genuine value — job search tips, market data, PM interview insights, or a product demo.
- **CTA:** One action only: "Link in bio to apply" / "Sign up free at scouterzero.com" / "Comment JOBS and I'll DM you."

### The 80/20 Rule

- **80% value content:** Job search tips, PM interview prep, market data, career advice, industry insights.
- **20% product content:** ScouterZero features, specific job listings, recruiter reveals, platform walkthroughs.

### Content Types

**Instagram Reels (Intern 1 — primary output):**
- Speaker-to-camera: personal story, job tip, market insight (15–45 sec)
- Screen recording of ScouterZero in action (recruiter reveal moment is the hook)
- Text-on-screen: fast-paced stats or tips (low production cost, Higgsfield or Canva)
- AI avatar option (once Higgsfield trial is live): scale volume without filming every day

**Carousels (Intern 1):**
- Structure: Problem → Insight → Solution → Proof → CTA
- 7–10 slides max. Large text, minimal clutter.
- Topics: "5 PM jobs hiring right now in Dubai," "Why your PM applications get no response," "How to contact a recruiter directly"

**LinkedIn Posts — Founder Voice (Aniket, daily):**
- Personal narrative + data point + strong opinion
- Format: 3–5 short paragraphs. First line is the hook. No fluff. End with a question or CTA.
- AI drafts; Aniket edits and posts. This is non-negotiable — the founder voice is our most credible distribution channel.
- Target: 1 post/day, minimum 5/week

**Community Posts (Intern 2):**
- WhatsApp groups, Discord servers, Reddit threads
- Give value 4x before mentioning ScouterZero
- Script templates for each platform

### AI-Augmented Content Production (New in V3)

The entire content pipeline is AI-assisted. The human adds taste, judgment, and personal story. The AI handles drafts, formats, and schedules.

**Script generation:** Claude (with a pre-loaded ScouterZero project: platform features, target audience, tone guide, past top-performing posts).
- Prompt via Super Whisper (voice dictation) to draft faster. Speaking generates 2–3x more context than typing.

**Video production:** Higgsfield (primary), VO3 or Nano Banana for experimental ad-style content.

**SEO articles:** Outrank or Claude — daily LLM-optimised articles targeting PM job search queries.

**Scheduling:** n8n workflow — auto-post to Instagram and LinkedIn on schedule.

### Product-Led SEO Strategy (New in V3)

ScouterZero already has location landing pages (/dubai, /london, /new-york, /usa, /remote). These are SEO gold. They need content depth.

**Target query categories:**
- "PM jobs in Dubai 2026"
- "How to find product manager jobs in London"
- "Remote PM jobs with salary"
- "How to contact a recruiter directly on LinkedIn"
- "Best job boards for product managers"

**Tactics:**
- Intern 3 uses Outrank or Claude to generate 2–3 SEO articles per week, each targeting one of these query clusters.
- Content is published on the ScouterZero blog or as expanded sections within location landing pages (coordinate with Aniket on implementation).
- Each article ends with a CTA to visit the job board.

> PLG principle applied: Canva acquires millions of monthly users by creating free tools that rank for high-volume job-to-be-done queries ("build an Instagram post"). ScouterZero does the same with "find a PM job in [city]."

From the PLG and AI research: "There is a huge arbitrage opportunity right now for people who are using AI to do marketing." The goal is not to automate everything — it's to automate every task that doesn't require human judgment, so the humans can focus on what only they can do: strategy, taste, and relationships.

## 11. Content Quality Standards (New in V3)

This section is Aniket's review checklist before anything goes live.

### Hook Test
- Does the first line/frame stop the scroll without being clickbait?
- Is the tension or benefit clear within 3 seconds?

### Value Test
- Does this content make a PM job seeker meaningfully better informed or better positioned?
- Is there a concrete insight, not just a vague observation?

### Taste Standard (from PLG research)
- Is the visual clean? One message per slide/frame.
- No typos. No broken formatting. Zero tolerance for obvious errors.
- Does the CTA match the content? (Don't push sign-ups on awareness-only content.)

### Brand Voice
- Confident but not arrogant
- Practical over inspirational
- Founder-authentic (Aniket's voice on LinkedIn should sound like a person, not a brand)

## 13. A/B Testing & Experimentation Framework (New in V3)

From the experimentation research: "There is a direct correlation between the number of experiments you run annually and your revenue growth."

We are not a large team. We run simple experiments. But we run them continuously.

### What We Test

| Element | How We Test |
|---|---|
| Instagram reel hooks | Post 2 versions of a similar hook in the same week; compare view rate |
| Carousel first slide | A/B two headlines on similar topic carousels; compare save rate |
| Landing page headline | Two versions of scouterzero.com hero headline; track sign-in rate (coordinate with Aniket) |
| Community post format | Link post vs. text-only post in same community; compare clicks |
| LinkedIn post CTA | Hard CTA vs. question-based ending; compare engagement |

### Rules
- Test one variable at a time
- Run for at least 5 days before drawing conclusions
- Log every test in a dedicated "Experiments" tab in the metrics Google Sheet: Hypothesis | Variable | Variation A | Variation B | Metric | Result | Decision
- **Ship early, iterate fast.** "Fast gets good quicker than good gets fast."

## 14. PLG Flywheel: How ScouterZero Grows

Understanding our own growth mechanics helps us know what to push on.

```
Awareness (Instagram, LinkedIn, SEO, Community)
      |
      v
Website Visit (location landing pages as SEO entry points)
      |
      v
Free Experience — 2 LinkedIn clicks free (REVERSE TRIAL IN ACTION)
      |
      v
Gate Hit — recruiter email or 3rd LinkedIn click
      |
      v
Google Sign-in (ACTIVATION = our North Star)
      |
      v
Newsletter opt-in (RETENTION hook)
      |
      v
Return visits / word of mouth (EXPANSION)
```

Every marketing activity should be evaluated against this flywheel. Does it drive more people to the top? Does it remove friction in the middle? Does it build retention at the bottom?
